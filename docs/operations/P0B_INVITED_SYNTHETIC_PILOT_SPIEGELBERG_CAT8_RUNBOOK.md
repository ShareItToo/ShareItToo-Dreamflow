- Germany, exact municipality `Spiegelberg, Rems-Murr-Kreis`, internal region
  code `spiegelberg`;

  multi-provider projects, external AI, public registration or real money.

The code `spiegelberg` is a recommendation, not current configuration.

## Mandatory prerequisite evidence

Do not begin participant setup until all four machine-bound results are true:

1. independent professional legal approval;
2. real operator roles, delegates, company RBAC/MFA and human 72-hour absence
   tests;
3. current-source signed and physical Android plus iOS device evidence; and
4. contracted marketplace-PSP sandbox E2E passed.

A partial Android pass, synthetic staffing rehearsal or local payment test does
not satisfy the corresponding prerequisite.

## Controlled preparation after eligibility

Only after the prerequisite gate turns green:

1. approve a privacy-minimized roster system outside Git; use opaque cohort
   references in repository evidence;
2. verify adulthood and private-use declarations without copying identity
   documents into Git, chat or push notifications;
3. provision isolated test accounts and synthetic listings for the exact
   region/category scope;
4. verify `livemode=false`, public registration off and no external marketing,
   analytics or AI SDK activation;
5. rehearse Support/Trust/Payment/Privacy escalation with assigned delegates;
6. run a five-flow internal dry run, then controlled waves of invited users;
7. record sanitized aggregate evidence only; and
8. stop at 50 complete flows or earlier on a hard stop.

## Required flow coverage

The 30 to 50 flows collectively cover quote freshness, private declarations,
owner accept/decline/timeout, synthetic payment success/failure, cancellation,
withdrawal, four-photo handover and return, missing confirmation,
substantiated `needsReview`, separated refund/payout states, messaging,
support escalation, account export and deletion. Test payments must be clearly
labelled and must never be represented as real money.

## Targets, not facts

- AOV target band: EUR 45 to 55;
- successful handovers: greater than 95 percent;
- severe disputes: less than 2 percent; and
- 90-day repeat booking: at least 25 percent.

Before observation these values are targets only. Missing, incomplete or
denominator-unsafe data is reported as unavailable, never as zero or success.

## Hard stops

Stop participant activity on any live key or real-money signal, public signup,
scope/category/region drift, missing assigned on-call role, provider/DB
mismatch, duplicate money action, sensitive-data leak, unsafe physical event,
unavailable evidence chain, severe systemic defect or legal/provider gate
regression. Preserve sanitized evidence and do not add replacement users past
the exact cohort cap without a new decision.

## Completion boundary

A completed synthetic pilot still does not authorize public registration,
production, real money, Store release or geographic/category expansion. Those
require later source-bound decisions.
