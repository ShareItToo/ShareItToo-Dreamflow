## Result

WP78 creates a new, separately versioned V5.3 draft legal map. It covers all
nine user-facing subjects from V5.2 but binds them to the confirmed sole
proprietor facts without modifying V5/V5.2 historical evidence. The source is
`assets/legal/de/legal_manifest_v53.json` (SHA-256
`d1265d31f68a7c616d782a78cd77c22eadb03dabbbd7e9ebe36a485d6d1a49c6`).

The V5.3 validator checks every document hash, the historical V5.2 manifest
hash, exact operator facts, nine-part topology, legacy company-fact rejection,
and fail-closed tax, payment, DSA, public-release and Maps boundaries. The
legal readiness validator also binds V5.3's manifest SHA into the current
draft readiness record.

## Holds

V5.3 is not a professional legal approval. It does not enable public operation,
registration, invitations, contract acceptance, money, PSPs, Store,
Production, public legal routes or Maps configuration. It does not assume a
tax treatment or publish an invoice, tax, register or representative fact.

The VSBG, DSA, privacy, retention/deletion, provider/DPA/transfer, consumer,
contract, payment/refund and public-delivery questions remain open gates. The
existing public legal runtime mismatch remains WP77 and is not altered by this
repository-only draft.
