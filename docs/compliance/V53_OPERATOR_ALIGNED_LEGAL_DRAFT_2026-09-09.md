public route, a legal approval, a contract acceptance mechanism or a release
authorization. V5 and V5.2 remain preserved historical evidence; V5.3 does not

## Tax, consumer and DSA boundaries

V5.3 does not assume a tax treatment or offer invoice logic. Any later tax
classification and the resulting payment, receipt or disclosure logic must be
professionally confirmed. It does not link the discontinued EU ODR platform.

The VSBG treatment is deliberately differentiated: the general website/terms
information question depends on the actual employee count and participation
position at the relevant time; information after an unresolved individual
consumer dispute remains a separate process to assess and implement. The DSA
micro/small-enterprise rule is not represented as a general exemption. DSA
classification, authority and recipient contact points, terms transparency and
notice-and-action remain independent legal and implementation gates.

## Required closure before use

Professional review remains required for operator/imprint completeness,
business registration, tax and invoices, consumer/marketplace classification,
acceptance/cancellation/withdrawal, payment provider/refund flow,
privacy/retention/deletion, DPA/transfers, DSA and public delivery. The Maps
architecture stays server-side-key-only; any future restriction needs the
confirmed VPS egress IP and exactly the required Places API. No Android-embedded
key, Maps change or provider activation is authorized by this draft.
